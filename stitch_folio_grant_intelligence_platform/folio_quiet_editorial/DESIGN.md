## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, composure, and timeless authority of an independent editorial press fused with modern intelligence tooling. Designed for foundation leaders, program officers, and institutional grantmakers, the interface deliberately strips away the frantic cadence and neon accents typical of conventional enterprise software. 

The aesthetic is grounded in tactile minimalism, drawing inspiration from archival broadsheets, fine stationery, and curated literary dossiers:
- **Tone:** Measured, intellectual, transparent, and quietly confident. It feels human, unhurried, and deeply dependable.
- **Physicality:** Evokes high-grade unbound paper stocks, pressed ink marks, subtle card stratification, and deckle-inspired surface offsets rather than synthetic glass or plastic depth.
- **Visual Discipline:** Zero synthetic blues, zero purple gradients, and zero decorative AI sparkles. Complexity is tamed through generous negative space, deliberate horizontal rhythm, and authoritative typographic hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette is built upon layered ivory papers, pressed botanical greens, and archival terra cotta ink accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Background Canvas: `#FDFBF7` (warm ivory)
  - Surface Mid: `#F7F4EE` (soft cream document sheets)
  - Surface Recessed: `#EFECE4` (structural backdrops, secondary panels)
  - Sheet Elevated: `#FFFFFF` (crisp paper white for active cards and dossier pages)
  - Architectural Borders: `#E4DFD3` (subtle framing) and `#DDD7CA` (structural dividers)

- **Text & Ink Hierarchy:**
  - Ink Primary: `#1C1D1B` (deep mineral charcoal, non-synthetic)
  - Ink Secondary: `#2C2D2A` (soft charcoal for body and metadata)
  - Ink Muted: `#63625D` (faded editorial ink for footnotes, timestamps, and captions)

- **Accents:**
  - Primary Green: `#1E382B` (deep forest green for principal actions, focused tabs, verified marks)
  - Primary Green Hover: `#15291F`
  - Primary Green Tint: `#E8EFEA` (selected row backgrounds, subtle badges)
  - Accent Terracotta: `#D96B43` (muted coral/terracotta for highlights, alerts, and critical funding signals)
  - Accent Terracotta Hover: `#C85A32`
  - Accent Terracotta Wash: `#F9EBE6` (contextual alerts and notice cards)

## Typography

The type scale creates an intentional juxtaposition between an authoritative literary serif (`Newsreader`) and a clean, warm humanist sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):

- **Headlines & Expressive Metrics:** Rendered in `Newsreader` with optical sizing adjustments. Titles evoke long-form investigative journalism and academic treatises. Large numeric indicators (e.g., funding disbursements, match allocations) use serif styling with italicized currency markers to maintain document-like gravitas.
- **Body & Continuous Reading:** Handled by `Plus Jakarta Sans` at comfortable reading line heights (1.6x - 1.55x). Provides exceptional clarity across long-form mission statements and programmatic dossiers.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Set in uppercase, tracked-out `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to act as clean index tags, card classification markers, and status indicators without overpowering the content.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an asymmetrical 12-column editorial grid for desktop and an adaptive 4-column system for mobile:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Employs a broad margin (`margin`: `3rem`) and spacious gutters (`gutter`: `1.5rem`). Content does not edge-to-edge stretch; views are capped at a maximum readability width of `1360px` with intentional wide margins for dossier annotations and archival indices.
- **Mobile / Narrow Views:** Collapses to single or dual stacked columns with disciplined padding (`margin-mobile`: `1.25rem`). Data displays avoid horizontal clipping through vertical definition lists instead of congested data tables.
- **Negative Space Philosophy:** Whitespace is treated as active paper margin rather than empty void. Vertical spacing between thematic blocks leans heavily on `space-xl` (`3.5rem`) to enforce a calm, meditative reading posture.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic drop-shadows, blurred overlays, or heavy colored tints. Instead, depth is achieved through physical sheet layering and subtle deckle-paper offsets:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Tier 0 (Backing Canvas):** `#FDFBF7` serves as the drafting table.
  - **Tier 1 (Dossier Sheets & Table Modules):** `#F7F4EE` framed with hairline borders (`#E4DFD3`, `1px solid`).
  - **Tier 2 (Active Paper Cards & Floating Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` paper surfaces with an ultra-soft, warm physical shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 45, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(44, 45, 42, 0.02)`.
  - **Tier 3 (Modals & Deep Review Sliders):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with an ivory scrim backdrop (`rgba(239, 236, 228, 0.75)` with `2px` blur) and an archival page drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(28, 29, 27, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(28, 29, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Connecting Line Metaphor:** Relational trees (parent foundations to grantee cohorts) employ 1px hairline lines (`#DDD7CA`) with sharp right-angle junctions rather than curved vector ribbons, maintaining an authentic archival lineage.

## Shapes

The shape geometry reflects traditional paper finishing—cut clean with lightly softened corners (`0.25rem` / `4px` base) to avoid harshness while resisting playful or bubbly consumer geometry:

- **Base Curvature:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) applied to cards, data blocks, text fields, and buttons.
- **Badges and Pills:** Compact chips and status indices may use soft capsules (`rounded-full`) purely to distinguish metadata tags from structural paper cards.
- **Borders:** Crisp, razor-thin lines (`1px`) utilizing natural paper-aging tones (`#E4DFD3` and `#DDD7CA`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest green fill (`#1E382B`), crisp paper white text (`#FFFFFF`), `4px` border radius, padded with `0.75rem` vertical and `1.25rem` horizontal. Hover state deepens to `#15291F` with a subtle `0.5px` ink shift.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base with hairline border (`1px solid #DDD7CA`), dark charcoal text (`#1C1D1B`). Hover adopts surface `#F7F4EE` and border `#1E382B`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Unbordered Newsreader or Sans link, underlined with a light terracotta line (`#D96B43`, `1px`) offset by `3px`.

### Cards & Dossier Sheets
- Rendered in crisp paper white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft cream (`#F7F4EE`), bounded by a subtle `1px` border in `#E4DFD3`.
- Internal card headers combine uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm` in `#63625D`) with an editorial serif title (`headline-sm` or `headline-md`).

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF` (or `#FDFBF7` in dense views) with border `1px solid #DDD7CA`, rounded `4px`, text in `#1C1D1B`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions cleanly to `#1E382B` without glowing outer rings. A hairline interior ring (`0 0 0 1px #1E382B`) preserves crispness.
- **Placeholder:** `#63625D` in italicized or low-contrast human sans.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags:** Background tinted in muted terracotta (`#F9EBE6`) with `#C85A32` text for urgent grant actions, or soft botanical wash (`#E8EFEA`) with `#1E382B` text for approved/verified entries.
- **Filter Tags:** Flat `#EFECE4` background, charcoal text (`#2C2D2A`), hover background `#E6E2D8`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with `2px` corners or circular rings. Border `1.5px solid #DDD7CA`. Checked state displays filled `#1E382B` background with a clean paper-white checkmark or central pip.

### Relationship Trees & Grant Timelines
- Visual nodes connecting funding organizations to regional initiatives use thin `#DDD7CA` rectilinear connecting tracks. Nodes consist of 8px square stamps or circular tokens filled with `#1E382B` or `#D96B43`.

### Expressive Stat Blocks
- Minimal container with no decorative graphics. Features a massive serif figure (`metric-numeral`) seated above a hairline separator (`#E4DFD3`), anchored beneath by a concise, uppercase descriptive metric label in muted ink.
